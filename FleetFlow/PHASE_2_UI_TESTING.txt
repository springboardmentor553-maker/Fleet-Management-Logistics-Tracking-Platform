SUMMARY OF RESPONSIVE IMPLEMENTATION
================================================================
1. Drawer Sidebar System (App.jsx & App.css):
   - Off-canvas sidebar on ≤768px (`left: -240px`)
   - Animated slide-in (`left: 0`) when hamburger `☰` is clicked
   - Dark blurred backdrop overlay (`.sidebar-overlay`)
   - Auto-closes on navigation item click

2. Modal & Form Grid Protection:
   - Replaced all inline `gridColumn: span 2` with `.full-width-field`
   - `@media (max-width: 600px)` resets `.full-width-field` to 1-column span
   - Clamped modals to `max-width: min(600px, calc(100vw - 24px))` with vertical scroll (`max-height: 88vh`)
   - Inputs, selects, textareas clamped to `max-width: 100%` with `box-sizing: border-box`

3. Data Tables:
   - Wrapped all tables in `.table-wrap` with `overflow-x: auto`
   - Clean horizontal scrolling on narrow viewports without page layout breakage
